# Licence of the VALDO training data -- read on 2026-09-20 (Claude)

Source: Zenodo dataset "Training dataset for the VALDO 2021 challenge", https://zenodo.org/records/4520773 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4520773),
read out via a reading model; the challenge page valdo.grand-challenge.org/Data/ returns HTTP 403 without login.

- **Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)** -- NOT just CC BY-NC, as previously stated in our
  notes. Redistribution and adaptations are permitted if non-commercial, with attribution AND under the same licence.
- Required according to the entry: acknowledgment of the funders (Wellcome Trust, British Heart Foundation, Erasmus MC, among others); when using
  Task 2: "for the ALFA Study" as corporate author and the 29 contributors named there; citation of the cohort papers
  (SABRE: Tillin et al. 2012; Rotterdam Scan Study: Ikram et al. 2015) and of the dataset via DOI.
- Task 2 (microbleeds, 2.8 GB) is part of the entry.

Consequences for us: a public page with VALDO slices (QC page, lecture page) is legally possible under the licence, if it
(1) is non-commercial, (2) carries the attribution including DOI, cohort citations and ALFA addendum, and (3) is itself under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
The existing pages name only "CC BY-NC 4.0" and the challenge article -- adjust before a publication.
Not checked: any additional conditions on the challenge page (readable only with login).
