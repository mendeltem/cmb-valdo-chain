# Literature scan: methods for very small brain lesion segmentation/detection

Interim version (draft, being extended with more sources). Budget: web search/fetch only, abstracts/READMEs preferred.
Context: our pipeline = anisotropic 3D U-Net (1x3x3 first level), T2*+FRST channels, BCE+Dice, AdamW+cosine, 60 epochs,
60/25/15 patch sampling, InstanceNorm, threshold+min-size+SynthSeg-CSF postproc. See system context for full "already tested" list.

## Top 8 untested ideas for our pipeline (ranked)

1. **blob loss / instance-wise loss (blob loss, CC-DiceCE, ICI, or CATMIL auxiliary term)** in place of or added to our BCE+Dice — directly targets the instance-imbalance problem of many tiny separate CMBs, which plain Dice does not; at least one variant (dawai) already benchmarked on our exact VALDO cohort. Cheap-medium to test (loss-function swap only).
2. **Synthetic CMB insertion (GAN-generated or copy-paste "ecological" lesion insertion into intact brain)** as augmentation — evidence of clearly beating standard augmentation (84% vs ≤60% sensitivity at matched FP rate) for CMB specifically; different mechanism from the z-score+basic-augmentation we already found hurts.
3. **High-pass-filtered phase image as a second, physically distinct discriminator channel** (beyond FRST) for calcification-vs-blood differentiation — addresses a mimic class (calcification) our SynthSeg-CSF rule does not cover; needs phase data availability check.
4. **Anatomical-location prior (deep GM / lobar / infratentorial, via SynthSeg regions already computed) as explicit FP-reduction feature or postproc rule**, extending our current CSF-only rule — one paper reports FP/subject 14.73→0.56 (unverified magnitude, but the mechanism is cheap to add given we already run SynthSeg).
5. **Differential CMB-vs-iron-deposit modeling (DEEPMIR-style)** — basal ganglia iron deposits are a classic mimic distinct from CSF false positives; not covered by our current postproc.
6. **Resampling to isotropic resolution + N4 + z-score "funnel" harmonization (SHIVA-CMB style) as a combined preprocessing bundle**, rather than z-score alone (which we found hurts) — testing the combination (not just the norm step in isolation) is untested by us.
7. **Light fine-tuning after explicit harmonization** (vs. our already-tested plain fine-tuning/joint/target-only) — MS-lesion domain-adaptation literature suggests harmonization-before-fine-tune as a distinct variant from fine-tuning on raw pooled data.
8. **nnDetection / Retina-U-Net-style detection head (box or point regression from decoder features) as an alternative or complementary output to voxel segmentation** — reframes CMB finding as detection, sidestepping the small-instance-swamped-by-Dice problem structurally rather than via loss weighting; higher engineering cost, hence ranked last.
