# What options remain? (user question 2026-09-28 after freezing the VALDO chain)

Standard (WORKLOG.md 5q, 5ac): an idea only counts if it gives the network DIFFERENT information or changes
the training distribution. Anything that only makes the network more sensitive has been measured and brings
no F1. The measured weakness (5ad): small (< 10 mm3), low-contrast bleeds under massive burden are not seen
at all (maximum probability < 0.05).

## C Internally still-open ideas (from WORKLOG.md, never measured)

- **Cubic interpolation / training at native resolution (4.8):** resampling to 0.5 x 0.5 x 1 mm costs a measured
  15-30% lesion contrast, most in the thick-slice cohorts -- sub-110 is cohort 1 (4 mm) and fails on low-contrast
  lesions. z = 2 mm was negative (-0.036 n.s.), cubic is unmeasured. Caution per 5ac: a CPU contrast measurement
  MUST NOT pre-decide this run.
- **T1 + T2 as channels (5q e):** upper bound of the single-sequence solution, VALDO only; catalina has no T1.
- **Artificial bleeds and mimics (5q c):** the plan was "only for the classifier"; A1 shows the gain instead in
  the FIRST stage (sensitivity), because that is where the examples are missing.
- **mIP as a channel (5q b):** cheap (minimum over ±2 slices on the grid), without literature support; a 3D
  network already sees the z context.
- **Phase:** VALDO has none, catalina has none locally (checked 28 Sep). Can only be obtained retroactively via
  PACS export.
- **Retraining the delivery model on all 72 VALDO cases:** +26% training data, no longer measurable (the 15 are
  used up); common and sensible for the delivered model, but the paper figure remains that of the CV/held-out set.

## D Ranking (yield per effort, as of 28 Sep)

1. **Artificial small, low-contrast bleeds + artificial mimics into the FIRST stage** (A1). The only lever with a
   gain ON VALDO (+2.6 points over the other levers, overall 65.8 -> 74.3) and the only one that hits the measured
   weakness: the network has almost no training examples for massive burden of small low-contrast bleeds
   (cohort 1: three CV cases above 5). New information = mimic examples; this is what distinguishes it from
   blob/centre, which only made it more sensitive. Effort: several days of code (Gaussian lesion per Momeni,
   contrast drawn from our own contrast distribution, location in white matter/cortex via SynthSeg; mimics as
   short dark cylinders = vessel cross-sections), two training runs with two seeds. Measure ONLY in the CV,
   paired, rule set in advance: a gain only counts above 0.08 (noise level) or matching across two seeds.
2. **Cubic interpolation of the image** (C): recompute one grid, one training run; direct answer to "contrast
   loss from resampling". Small, but cheap.
3. **Obtain the Momeni/CSIRO SWI set** (B): check licence and image parameters; if free, use as an external test
   of the SWI model (first independent public figure) and as a training increment for SWI. No GPU effort for the
   check.
4. **T1/T2 upper bound** (paper figure only, 2 GPU hours).
5. **mIP channel** (one training run, low expectation).
6. **Phase/QSM**: strongest different information (A4), but without data. Note for the next data collection.

Not recommended: pseudo-labels (no CMB evidence), foundation models (no number for lesions of this size), further
architectures (5s), further variants of the second stage (5r, 5z, 5ab, 5y). Precondition for everything: the
blinded expert judgement (5b) -- if many "false positives" are real bleeds, precision is underestimated and the
ranking above shifts toward sensitivity.
